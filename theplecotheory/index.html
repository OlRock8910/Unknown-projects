Looking after them

🌡️ 18–26°CRoom temperature is usually fine. A heater helps in a cold room.
💧 pH 6.5–8Normal UK tap water works once it's dechlorinated.
⏳ Acclimatise slowlyFloat the bag, then add a little tank water every 10 minutes for about an hour.
🚫 No copperMany plant and snail treatments contain copper, which kills shrimp.
