## Full description
Climb ten real mountains and learn a language on the way up.

Lingo Peak teaches Spanish, French, German, Brazilian Portuguese and English,
from whichever of those you already speak. Each mountain, from Snowdon to
Everest, has five units of everyday words and sentences. Every lesson takes you
higher, and each mountain stays hidden in the clouds until you reach the top.

**Learn the way memory works**
• Spaced repetition: words come back just before you'd forget them
• You recall words, not just re-read them, so they stick
• Every word is spoken by a natural voice
• Speak answers out loud, fill gaps in sentences and build your own sentences
• A coach that spots your weak points and tells you what to practise next

**Characters who talk back**
A banana, a shark, a dinosaur and friends in costumes say things in your new
language. Tap any word to see what it means, then work out the whole sentence.

**Just 20 minutes a week**
Set a weekly goal of 10 to 45 minutes and keep your weekly streak going. A
home-screen widget shows how close you are.

**Rewards that feel good**
• Gold sparks and a big "da-dun" for every right answer
• Coins and XP for every lesson
• A daily chest, streak rewards and 19 achievements
• A badge for every mountain you climb
• Clothes, hats and power-ups for your climber

**Play with friends**
Weekly, all-time and friends leaderboards. Add friends with a code.

**13 mini games**
Read Aloud (read a paragraph, words light up green as you say them), Bubble
Pop, Whack-a-Word, Memory, Word Rain, Quick Quiz and more.

Free to learn. Light and dark themes.
