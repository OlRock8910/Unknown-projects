# Lingo Peak privacy policy

_Last updated: 18 October 2026_

Lingo Peak is a language-learning app. This page explains what it stores,
where, and how to delete it.

## What the app stores

**On your phone:** your progress (lessons, words, coins, XP, streaks,
achievements), your character and your settings. This stays on your phone.

**Online, so leaderboards and backups work:**

- an account ID, made automatically the first time you're online;
- your **username** and your **character's look**, which other players see on
  the leaderboards;
- your learning stats: XP, words learned, weekly streak, badges and the
  language you're learning, also shown on the leaderboards;
- a backup copy of your progress, which only you can read;
- your friends list, if you add friends with a friend code;
- your **email address**, only if you choose **Save your progress** or **Log in
  with email**. It's used to send you log-in codes and nothing else. Other
  players never see it.

**Speaking exercises:** when you answer by speaking, your phone's speech
recognition (on Android, Google's) turns what you said into text. Lingo Peak
only keeps whether the answer was right. It doesn't keep any recordings.

## What the app does not do

- No adverts, no ad tracking and no analytics.
- Your data is never sold or shared for marketing.
- No payment details pass through Lingo Peak. If coin packs are added, Google
  Play handles the payment.

## Where it's kept

Online data is stored with [Supabase](https://supabase.com), which runs the
app's database. Log-in code emails are sent through Gmail.

## Deleting your account

In the app: **Me → ⚙ Settings → Delete account** (at the very bottom). You
confirm with a code sent to your email, or by typing your username. This
deletes your account and everything stored online straight away.

You can also ask for your account to be deleted by emailing
**[CONTACT EMAIL]** from the address on the account, or with your username.
It's done within 7 days.

## Children

Lingo Peak is for learners of all ages. Children under 13 should use it with a
parent's permission. Saving progress to an email is optional.
