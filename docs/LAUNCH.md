## Short video (TikTok / Reels / Shorts) — this is the one that spreads

Screen-record yourself playing (Android: swipe down › Screen record, sound on
so the Spanish voice is in it). 15–25 seconds. Ideas that tend to work:

1. **"Describing my family in Spanish"** — play Gender Pop or Describe Them.
   On-screen text: *my brother = molesto 😤 my sister = mandona 👑 me =
   perezoso 🦥*. End on the big word flash.
2. **"Can you get 3 stars?"** — a fast Bubble Pop round, end on the results
   screen. Caption: *bet you can't beat 300 🫧 link in bio*.
3. **"Spanish words that sound like insults"** — *pesado*, *creído*,
   *chismoso*, *dormilón*. Show each one flashing up big.
4. **Duet/stitch bait** — "Say the Spanish before the bubble pops 👀".

Caption: `Learning Spanish adjectives with mini games 🇪🇸 free, no ads, link in bio #spanish #learnspanish #languagelearning #spanishlearning #studytok`

Put the browser link in your bio — it plays instantly, no install.

## On GitHub

- Repo › ⚙️ **Settings › General › Social preview**: upload `web/og-image.png`.
- Repo home page › ⚙️ next to **About**: add the website link and topics
  `spanish`, `language-learning`, `education`, `game`, `android`, `pwa`.

  get a proper download button.

## Keep it going

- Ask people which words to add next, and add them (`web/js/data.js`).
- Post a new short whenever you add a game.
